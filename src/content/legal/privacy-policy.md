# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 2026_

This site is the personal portfolio of Gavin Pereira. It is a small site, and
this policy describes in plain terms what it does and does not collect.

## What is collected

**The contact form.** When you send a message, the name, email address and
message you type are transmitted to the mail service that delivers it. That
information is used to reply to you and nothing else. It is not sold, rented or
shared with advertisers.

**Spam protection.** The contact form uses Google reCAPTCHA to tell people from
bots. Google receives the information needed to run that check, subject to
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms).

**Hosting and delivery.** Like every website, the host and the CDN that serves
images record standard request data such as IP address, browser user agent and
the page requested. This is ordinary server logging, not profiling.

## What is stored in your browser

The site stores a single preference in `localStorage`: whether you chose the
light or the dark theme. It contains no identifier, it never leaves your
device, and clearing site data removes it.

There are no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics scripts.

## Third-party services

- **ImageKit** — delivers the portfolio images and videos.
- **Google reCAPTCHA** — protects the contact form from automated abuse.
- **The hosting provider** — serves the site itself.

Each of these receives only what it needs to do its job.

## Links to other sites

Project links, client sites and social profiles point to services that are not
operated by this site and have their own privacy practices. This policy does
not cover them.

## Your choices

You may ask for any message you have sent through the contact form to be
deleted, or ask what has been retained. Write to
[connect@gavn.in](mailto:connect@gavn.in) and it will be handled.
